# Product scope

## Purpose and intended users

The prototype helps radiation-oncology operations teams, workflow analysts, and
authorized research staff inspect longitudinal operational state and historical
patterns. It is a research and demonstration system, not a clinical system of
record.

## In scope

- Provenance-preserving normalization of an approved RADCURE dataset copy.
- Clearly labeled synthetic operational workflow generation.
- Deterministic point-in-time workflow reconstruction and evidence retrieval.
- Explainable historical-patient similarity.
- Descriptive summaries of historical operational next-step patterns.
- Model-assisted tool orchestration and evidence-linked narrative synthesis.
- Quantitative evaluation of accuracy, evidence, safety, latency, and cost.

## Out of scope

- Diagnosis, staging decisions, or clinical interpretation.
- Treatment, fractionation, radiation-dose, or scheduling recommendations.
- Toxicity, survival, response, or other clinical-outcome prediction.
- Autonomous clinical or operational decisions.
- Replacement of source records, institutional policies, or professional review.
- Any claim that Amazon Connect Health Patient Insights is integrated.
